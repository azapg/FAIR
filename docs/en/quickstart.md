## Open FAIR

Use the FAIR link provided by your school, instructor, or administrator.

Sign in with your email and password, or choose **Continue with Google** when
available. If you received an invitation, open its link to create your account.
See [Google sign-in](/en/platform/integrations) to connect an existing account.

## Find your courses

Open **Courses** to find your classes, assignments, and materials. If a course
is missing, ask your instructor or administrator to check your access.

- **Students:** open an assignment to read its instructions and submit your work.
- **Instructors:** open a course to manage assignments, participants, and grading.

Read the [course guide](/en/platform/courses) or
[assignment guide](/en/platform/assignments) for the next steps.

If you are setting up FAIR for yourself or your institution, use the
[installation guide](/en/development/installation) in **Developers & IT**.
