## Sign in with Google

Choose **Continue with Google** on the sign-in page and select your Google
account. The button appears when your school or FAIR administrator has enabled it.

If you need an invitation to join FAIR, open your invitation link first and
choose **Continue with Google** on the registration page. Use the Google account
with the same email as your invitation.

## Already have a FAIR account?

If your FAIR account uses the same email as your Google account, connect them once:

1. Sign in to FAIR with your email and password.
2. Open **Settings → Profile → Connect Google**.
3. Confirm your FAIR password and choose the Google account with that same email.

After connecting, you can sign in with either Google or your existing password.
Your courses, submissions, and permissions stay with the same FAIR account.

If you try Google before connecting it, FAIR asks you to follow these steps.
It does not create a second account or automatically join accounts by email.

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page when
available, or contact your FAIR administrator.

## Does this connect my courses?

Google sign-in gives you access to FAIR. Connecting Google Classroom courses or
opening FAIR from Moodle will be separate features; they are not available yet.
