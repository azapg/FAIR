**FAIR** helps instructors and students manage courses, share materials, submit
assignments, and review grades and feedback.

## Start here

- [Get started](/en/quickstart): sign in and find your courses.
- [Courses](/en/platform/courses): follow class activity and access materials.
- [Assignments](/en/platform/assignments): set work, submit it, and review feedback.
- [Google sign-in](/en/platform/integrations): use Google with a new or existing account.

## Optional AI tools

Your institution may enable tools for teaching, feedback, or research. FAIR is
also useful without AI, and instructors remain responsible for grading decisions.

FAIR is an open-source project from the [Fair Grade Project](https://fairgradeproject.org).
For installation, Google login setup, and technical guidance, open **Developers & IT**.
To build additional tools, use the [Extensions Reference](/en/extensions/overview).
